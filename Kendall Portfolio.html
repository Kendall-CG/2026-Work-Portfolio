About Me:

[ Profile ]

An E-Commerce & Marketing leader driving growth as one revenue engine.

I lead e-commerce and digital marketing as a single growth engine, and I thrive on the range it takes. I have owned a 200-SKU DTC storefront on WooCommerce, directed six agency partners across paid media, SEO, Amazon, and UX, and held every one accountable to ROAS and CAC. Promoted to Director of Digital Marketing & E-Commerce at Jonathan Green, I grew the storefront from $10K to $2.5M and drove a 45% net-sales lift.

Earlier, as an Associate Product Manager at Rocket Lawyer, I led a 15-person team building an e-commerce ecosystem of catalog, subscriptions, billing, and internal tools behind a multi-million-dollar subscription business. Today I'm Fractional Marketing Director of MDH Beauty.

Detail-oriented project management and an eye for design shape everything I ship, from merchandising and conversion to retention. Based in Austin, Texas, I recharge by cooking, traveling, and practicing yoga, and I bring that same curiosity and care to every team I join.
