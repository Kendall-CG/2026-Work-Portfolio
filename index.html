About Me

[ Profile ]

An E-Commerce & Marketing leader driving growth as one revenue engine.

I've spent my career at the point where brand, commerce, and the customer meet. At Jonathan Green, a national CPG brand, I set the direction for how we sold everywhere people shop: our own DTC storefront, Amazon Marketplace, and retail partners like Ace Hardware. I believe a brand should feel the same where it's in-store or online.

I lead by building systems that keep growing after launch: clear standards, honest measurement, and teams that know what they own. My product roots at Rocket Lawyer taught me to treat the storefront as a product, and today I bring that same thinking to brands as Fractional Director of Website & Marketing at MDH Beauty. I'm based in Austin, and I do my best work with leaders who want growth that lasts.
