Objective

Jonathan Green wasn't selling on Amazon directly, but our customers were. Third-party sellers had built listings for our products with outdated photos, thin content, and an all-around poor buying experience. With no uniform branding, imagery, or voice, those listings were quietly eroding the brand everywhere shoppers looked.

I initiated Brand Registry and pulled those listings into our own catalog, where I could own the quality. I consolidated duplicate listings, rebuilt detail pages, and ran keyword and market research so our products ranked higher for the searches that mattered, turning a fragmented presence into one of the brand's fastest-growing revenue streams.
