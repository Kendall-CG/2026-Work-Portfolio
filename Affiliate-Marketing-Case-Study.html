The business outcome.

Between January 2022 and December 2024, I transformed Jonathan Green's affiliate marketing program from a small, concentrated channel into a scalable acquisition engine generating more than $605K in attributed revenue.

The affiliate program relied heavily on a very small number of partners, creating significant concentration risk. Coupon sites dominated the mix, limiting profitability and average order value. Growth would require more than simply increasing commissions. It demanded rebuilding the channel from the ground up.

The Strategy

Five moves, one goal: profitable scale.

01

Partner Recruitment

Scaled the network from 22 to 167 partners while increasing active publishers from 7 to 56.

02

Commission Optimization

Redesigned tiered commission structures around incrementality instead of rewarding every transaction equally.

03

Partner Mix Optimization

Shifted investment away from coupon interception toward high-value gardening publishers and content creators.

04

Coupon Governance

Consolidated affiliate coupon codes into controlled program codes to eliminate leakage and measure profitability accurately.

05

Performance Analytics

Measured performance using fully-loaded CAC, ROAS, commission efficiency, and revenue contribution, not vanity metrics.

Why This Experience Matters

More than affiliate management: a scalable performance engine.

The Jonathan Green affiliate program demonstrates more than successful affiliate management. It shows the ability to build a scalable performance marketing engine. By recruiting higher-value partners, redesigning commission incentives, improving channel governance, and optimizing profitability, the program generated more than $604K in attributed revenue while reducing acquisition costs and improving return on every marketing dollar.

The most important outcome wasn't simply higher revenue. It was creating a sustainable, measurable growth channel that became increasingly efficient as it scaled.

I don't just grow affiliate programs. I build profitable partner ecosystems that scale.
